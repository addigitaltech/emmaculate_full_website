# Update 7C — A4 report card, passports, cumulative results, student PINs, fee-withheld list

## What changed
1. **A4 portrait report card** laid out like the school's reference sheet: logo and school details, name/gender, date of birth and age, class and arm, passport photograph, termly table, class performance graph, result analysis, skills and behaviour grid, teacher's and principal's comments. The PDF download and the public result-check PDF are now A4 portrait too.
2. **Passport photographs** — on the student form (Admin console > Students) use the **+** beside "Passport photograph" to upload straight from the page (JPEG/PNG/WebP, up to 2 MB). The photo shows on the student score sheet, the subject score sheet (small thumbnail) and the printed report (a labelled box when none is uploaded).
3. **Cumulative results (2nd and 3rd term)** — per subject: 1st / 2nd / 3rd term totals, cumulative total, cumulative average (2 decimals) and cumulative grade; the analysis adds cumulative total, average and **cumulative position** (ranked among every entered result of the class, rounded to 2 decimals). First-term reports are unchanged.
4. **Class teacher's name** appears on the signature line.
5. **Grading bug fixed** — a fractional score such as 69.5 (or 59.99, 49.5, 44.5, 39.5) used to fall in the gap between grade bands and be graded "F". Grades now use the highest band whose minimum the score reaches. Stored grades are corrected the next time a result is saved or published.
6. **Behaviour scale wording** now matches the reference sheet on both the entry page and the report: 1 Fair, 2 Pass, 3 Good, 4 Very good, 5 Excellent (previously 1 Poor ... 5 Excellent). Stored ratings keep their numbers 1–5.
7. **Student PIN sign-in (switchable)** — two new switches in Admin console > Portal settings (and the School settings page): **Require PIN to sign in (student portal)** and **Require PIN to check results (Check Result page)**. Both are **off by default**, so nothing changes for students until you switch them on. When a switch is on, that page needs admission number + surname + a **6-digit PIN**; when off, the PIN field is hidden and ignored. Only a hash of the PIN is stored. Wrong guesses are counted **per student whatever the IP address**: every 5th wrong PIN locks that student for 15 minutes, then 30, 60 ... up to 24 hours, until the correct PIN or a PIN reset. While a switch is on, students with no PIN cannot use that page until one is issued.
8. **Issuing PINs** — Admin console > Students: tick students and use **Issue PIN slips** (bulk), or the **PIN slip** button on one row. A new PIN replaces the old one, clears any lock, and a printable A4 PDF of cut-out slips downloads immediately. PINs are shown only on that PDF and cannot be looked up afterwards (issue a new one if lost). Needs the "manage students" permission; issuing is audit-logged (count only, never the PINs).
9. **Withheld-for-fees list** — on the Results page, administrators see "Reports withheld for unpaid fees" (only when "Hold results until school fees are paid" is on): students with published results this term who have unpaid or overdue fees, with items, amount due and a View report link. Grades and positions are calculated as normal for everyone; the fee gate only hides the report from the student and parent.

## Apply order
This ZIP is a catch-up bundle: it also carries the Update 7A files (CI workflow, integrity tests, unified results controller, updated StaffResultsTest, 7A notes). Update 7B must already be on main (it is live). Several files here also contain the 7A/7B changes.

## Database
Two additive migrations: four new columns on `students` (PIN hash, set time, failed attempts, lock time) and two new switches on `school_settings` (both off). Nothing is dropped. They run automatically on deploy.

## After deploying
Students sign in exactly as before until you switch the PIN on. Recommended order: (1) Students > filter by class > select all > **Issue PIN slips** and hand out the printed slips; (2) Portal settings > switch on **Require PIN to sign in** and/or **Require PIN to check results**; (3) if anything goes wrong, switch it off again and students can use admission number + surname straight away.
